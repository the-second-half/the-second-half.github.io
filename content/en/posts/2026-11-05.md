Two years of study, thinking, self-denial and starting over. Somewhere in there my head quieted down. Only at the end of 2025 did I begin to rebuild my own framework, my own view of life and of the world.

First: people do not die only when they are old. They can die at any time. So staying alive is the ultimate task — the absolute first priority.

Second: a life is a three-legged framework. Body, mind, money. All three legs have to be balanced, or the life falls over. If one leg breaks, the whole thing collapses. Living is just the endless work of balancing this framework and raising it a little higher.

The breakdown: taken from my own mid-life crisis, ordered from macro to micro, from theory to practice, from hard to easy, from urgent to what still has room.

1. **Layer one: body, mind and money in good shape** (the highest goal)
2. **Layer two: health, a settled mind, cash flow in balance**
3. **Layer three: lose weight, stay quiet, balance the household budget**
4. **Layer four: control what I eat, worry about fewer things, keep my salary income** (start now)

Rescue starts with the smallest thing I can actually do — climbing out of the mud one step at a time. Four layers, and each layer breaks down again into plans I can carry out.
