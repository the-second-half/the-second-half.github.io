In 2026 I lost forty jin in eight months. Every marker improved and most returned to normal. That was a real success. Along the way two side effects showed up: constipation and hair loss.

## 1. Constipation

Past forty-five the gut and the bowels start to decline, and after fifty it is more obvious. Losing weight makes it worse, because eating less stretches the interval between bowel movements. Before the diet I would go once every one or two days. After it started, two to three days became normal, sometimes three to four. In early August 2026 I ate a large amount of glutinous rice cakes (stuffed with plenty of preserved mustard greens) over several days, and on a business trip I did not drink enough water. That brought the first serious constipation of my life. It led directly to a perianal abscess and a fistula, and in late September I had to have surgery.

On constipation, my own experience plus what I read online suggests there are mature ways to prevent and handle it:

1. Eat plenty of fibre every day, both coarse and soluble. Mostly vegetables and coarse grains: greens, celery, okra, aubergine, konjac, sweet potato, potato, mushrooms, oats, and so on.

2. Drink a lot of water. (1) Warm water, 40–60 °C. (2) Plain water or light tea; avoid drinking large amounts of any sugary drink. (3) 2000–3000 ml a day. (4) A glass of warm water first thing every morning.

3. If two days pass with no clear urge and constipation feels close, take 30 ml of lactulose oral solution at once. I studied and personally tried many laxatives and supportive remedies; weighing the effect on the body against the result, what I recommend most is lactulose oral solution: (1) a single ingredient, it does not irritate the gut, no "melanosis coli" problem from long-term use, no residual toxins — better than Chinese medicine. (2) It tastes sweet but is not absorbed into the blood, so it has no effect on blood sugar. (3) The effect is gentle; as long as you do not overdo the dose it will not cause diarrhoea. (4) The price is moderate: a bottle of Duphalac costs about 53 yuan, and it can be prescribed at a hospital on medical insurance.

## 2. Hair loss

This is the problem that annoys and frustrates me most. Men start losing hair around fifty anyway, and cutting staples (carbohydrate) sharply to lose weight makes it much more likely. At the start of 2025 (age 49) I still had a fair amount of hair and you could not see scalp from outside. By the second half of 2026 it thinned fast, especially at the front — thin enough that it no longer covers the scalp. At this rate, by the first half of 2027 I will probably be bald in patches.

I read around. Some say it is telogen hair regrowing slowly, some say it is hormones at this age, some say it is lifestyle and diet. There is no good solution. I have started deliberately eating more staples and more carbohydrate, especially oats and tubers, and I now occasionally eat pork liver for iron — I used to refuse offal outright.

Let's see how it goes.

Floating, no shore. I am my own boat.
