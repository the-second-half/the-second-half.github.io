Subtitle: Up 2.1 kg in two days, back down in under 60 hours | A real trip, logged

Last Friday and Saturday I was in Shenzhen on business. Two days of back-to-back dinners with colleagues, and I drank and ate spicy food even though my anal fistula is still healing. From Sunday to Monday I worked on undoing it — mainly so that two bad days would not break a healthy rhythm I had spent two months building. Call it a 48-hour recovery window.

## 1. Two days away, two months of stability undone

For the week before the trip my weight sat between 74 and 75 kg. Two months of regular meals and daily walking had put it there. It was not easy to get.

Two days of dinners, a delayed flight and meals at random hours took it apart:

- Friday 13 November: flew to Shenzhen, flight delayed, first meal at 3 pm — a bowl of Meizhou noodles.
- Saturday 14 November: lunch ran into dinner, two bottles of beer, spicy dishes. The fistula was not going to stop me that day.
- To avoid constipation I overused a slimming/detox tablet — 20 on Friday, 10 on Saturday.
- Saturday's intake was about 3500 kcal, roughly 1800 above my 1700 kcal baseline.

The scale answered fast: 74.4 kg on Friday morning, 75.6 kg on Saturday morning. Back in Hefei on Saturday night: 76.5 kg.

## 2. What I actually did for 48 hours

| # | What | How |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Cut fat and sugar | Deliberately low fat, low sugar, to offset the surplus |
| 2 | Greens and water | Green vegetables and water at 1.5–2× normal, to clear sodium and sugar |
| 3 | More walking | Walking instead of running: 19.8 km Sunday, 7.1 km Monday |
| 4 | Stopped the tablets | I had taken them only against constipation; stopped on day two |
| 5 | Back to normal on day three | Emergency measures must not become the new normal |

## 3. The result: two days up, two days back

From the 76.5 kg peak on Saturday night to 74.6 kg on Tuesday morning — **down 1.9 kg in under 60 hours**.

More to the point, I am back where I started: 74.4 kg before the trip, 74.6 kg two days after coming home. A 0.2 kg difference. Two months of stability, intact.

## 5. One thing I have to say plainly

Sunday morning's 74.9 kg looks like the weight had already come back down. It had not. That was the laxative working overnight — water and gut contents, not fat. Which is why Sunday, on 1100 kcal and 28,000 steps, the scale still read 76.2 kg in the evening: the body was refilling water and glycogen.

I do not recommend the laxative route to anyone. It was a stopgap against constipation during the trip and I stopped it the next day. What actually brought the number down was the following two days of food and walking.

## 6. Fat or water?

7700 kcal is what it takes to make a kilogram of fat. Saturday's surplus was 1800 kcal — around 230 g of fat at the absolute maximum.

So of the 1.2–2.1 kg gained, fat accounts for a couple of hundred grams at most. The rest is water bound to glycogen (1 g of glycogen holds 3–4 g of water), sodium retention from salty restaurant food, the beer itself, and food still in transit.

None of that needs to be "burned off". It needs to be let go. Go back to normal eating and drinking and it leaves within 48–72 hours. That is what a recovery window really means.

## 7. In short

1. An occasional trip or blowout: give it two days, then go back to your normal rhythm.
2. A job that means constant dinners: at some point you have to choose between the job and your health.

Trips and dinners are not optional for me. What is optional is the two days after: no starving, no doubling the exercise, no laxatives — greens, water, walking. The body does the rest.
