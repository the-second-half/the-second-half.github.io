## What this is

I am in my early fifties, and I spent most of my career managing other people's businesses. Then my industry went into decline, the company I worked for collapsed, and my annual check-up started returning a row of arrows pointing the wrong way.

In two years I rebuilt three things at once: my body (95 kg to 75 kg), my mental state, and my finances. Not perfectly, and not in a straight line.

This site is the record. One entry a day, written the day it happened.

## Why make it public

Mostly for myself. Writing something down and putting it where other people can see it is a stronger commitment than a private notebook. That is the main reason.

The second reason is that a few people might find it useful. There is no shortage of content about losing weight, and no shortage of content about money. There is very little from someone in the middle of it, at this age, writing honestly about all three at once without selling anything.

## What you will not find here

- Before-and-after photos
- A method, a course, or a supplement
- Promises that it will not come back
- Anything about my employer, my former colleagues, or any specific company

## A note on privacy

This is written under a pen name. Not because the numbers are invented — they are exact, taken from my scale and my lab reports — but because the people around me did not agree to appear in public. The weight, the blood pressure, the food, the bad days: all real. The identity is not.

## Categories

- **Body** — weight, food, sleep, lab results, exercise
- **Mind** — mood, anxiety, motivation, what I tell myself
- **Money** — spending, saving, the psychology of a shrinking income
